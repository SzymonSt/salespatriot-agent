## Most useful findings

1. **Supplier search needs history.** "Who has supplied 9x19 ammunition to Polish buyers and at what unit price?"
   (Q08): without history the pack names none of the real suppliers (1/10: candidates are only the 20 profiled
   suppliers, none of which won these tenders). With history 6/10: `priors.baseline.top_winners` gives Cenzin,
   Archangelus, Mildat, Triplex, and the unit price 1.353 PLN (50 m rounds, ted:636930-2024) with a "weak prior,
   n = 1" warning. Ground truth also has Kaliber, RWS Cetus, DMA Puchalski and FAM-Pionki, which the pack misses.
2. **Quote checks only work with history.** For 1.60 PLN per 9x19 round (Q15) and 2,100 PLN per pistol (Q17) the
   baseline unit price is the only benchmark: "above p75" with history, "not benchmarked" without. Both rest on a
   single procedure, and the pack says so.
3. **The unit-price fix works.** POL 155 mm now returns no unit price and a warning instead of 3.95 bn PLN, so a
   15,000 PLN quote (Q16) is "not benchmarked" instead of "below p25" of a nonsense range. 5 suspect priors -> 1.
4. **Bid counts and Spanish 155 mm history.** Q03: `priors.baseline.bids_median = 1` for ESP 155 mm matches the
   ground truth (1 bid per procedure); evidence brings the 2023 and 2025 Spanish awards (Rheinmetall Expal, Fabrica de
   Municiones de Granada). Q10: two Slovenian ammunition suppliers (Group 22, Koptex) appear only through history.
5. **Semantic neighbours improve with history.** With the baseline, 46% of the 5 nearest past cards share the
   tender's product family vs 24% without: better for 8 of 14 tenders, equal for 4, worse for 2.

## Most problematic findings

1. **History crowds out more relevant live cards in fixed 8-slot lists.** `check_counterparty` lists the 8 newest
   cards; a baseline award card and a live contract card for the same notice take two slots (Q19 and Q20: 4 notices
   shown twice), so history adds older wins for Fiocchi (Q18 +1) but pushes one out for Transcarpat (Q20 -1). `search` ranks with `ts_rank_cd` and OR-ed terms, so common words win: "Simmunition training
   cartridges" (Q24) finds 3/3 without history and 1/3 with it, because baseline cards matching "training" or
   "cartridges" push out the two other Simmunition notices.
2. **Text search is effectively one-language.** `kb_search` uses `to_tsvector('simple')`: "81 mm mortar bombs" (Q23)
   finds 0 of 6 mortar notices (French and Swedish texts); the Lithuanian "12,7 x 99 mm šoviniai" (Q25) finds 4 of 9
   and misses the French, Greek and Polish 12.7x99 notices (439616-2026, 472813-2025, 569562-2025, 775493-2025,
   817388-2025). Without an API key the agents get no query embedding, so this is what they see.
3. **Embeddings cluster by language and buyer, not by calibre.** Only 2 of 70 nearest neighbours are in another
   language, and only 19% state the same calibre. Family precision counts unlabelled cards as misses: 7,902 of
   10,222 baseline procedures have no family (classification `none`), which also caps any family-filtered search.
4. **Several questions are not answered at all.** Q11 (who supplies ammunition to Estonia's RKIK): 0/8. The prior
   falls back to the whole `ammunition.medium_calibre` family (no country), and the buyer's `top_winners` are its
   all-category winners, mostly drone makers. Q13 (Slovak firearms) 1/6 and Q02 (French small-arms ammunition) 1/4
   for similar reasons: class-level or family-level priors, live data without these winners.
5. **Unknown values still reach the agent as numbers.** `bids_received = 0` on 177 of 3,357 awarded baseline
   procedures (0 means not reported: an award has at least one bid). Rheinmetall's contract lots for 601197-2026 and
   558036-2026 carry `awarded_value = -1` EUR (TED's "not published"). Both show up in evidence facts (7 packs).

## Caveats

- Ground truth is computed by our own SQL with CPV, buyer and title regex filters. It is independent of
  `agent_context`, but not of TED data quality, and winner names are matched by a distinctive token (e.g. "cenzin").
  Expected winners are the top winners by number of award notices in that scope, capped at 8.
- Recall says whether the information reached the pack, not whether an agent would use it well.
- The "without history" pack is an emulation (see README). It is exact for the evidence list (34/34 fidelity) and
  for the removed priors; it does not model what a future `agent_context` with a real layer switch might add instead.
- Q15-Q17 are emulated quote checks; Q14 is the only real `evaluate_quote` (one demo conversation exists).
- The "embed" mode uses a stored card embedding as a proxy for a query embedding; a real query is shorter.
- Plausibility bands and FX rates for "wrong numbers" are assumptions (generous, order-of-magnitude only).
- Latency was measured while other agents were building `baseline_v2` in the same database.
- Sample: 25 questions, 142 expected items. Differences of 1-2 items per question are within noise.
