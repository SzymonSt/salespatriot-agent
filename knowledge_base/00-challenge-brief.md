# Challenge brief — Build the Agent Marketplace

Source: "Build the Arsenal — SalesPatriot Europe Challenge" PDF (12 slides). `[VERIFIED 2026-10-30]`

## Event
- Build the Arsenal · Innovation Challenge, organised by SalesPatriot (YC W25).
- Warsaw, Poland Tech Week, Friday 30 October 2026. One day, one open challenge.
- SalesPatriot already uses AI to help suppliers win US defence work. The challenge: go
  further — a market where agents do the work, for Europe.

## The problem as the organisers frame it
1. **Fragmented** — buyers, primes and small suppliers are spread across countries, portals
   and languages, with no shared market.
2. **Manual** — finding a supplier, getting a quote or forming a team takes weeks of emails,
   calls and PDFs.
3. **Missed** — capable suppliers never meet the buyers who need them, so capacity sits idle
   while demand waits.

## The task
Build a market for the European supply chain where AI agents act for buyers and suppliers.
What they trade, and how, is up to us.

- **Supply** — anyone who can make or provide: companies, research labs, public bodies, and
  what they can do.
- **Demand** — anyone who needs it: governments, agencies, prime contractors, other
  suppliers, and what they need.
- **Deal** — supply meets demand and closes the deal, autonomously.

## Must have (a finished entry)
1. Live app demoed on the host's computer. No slides, no video.
2. Real public data from European sources.
3. Both sides of the market, and **at least one deal closed with no human in the loop**.

## Ground rules
1. Include real, live public data. **No export-controlled (ITAR/EAR), classified, CUI or
   customer data.**
2. Respect the sites: follow site terms, don't hammer servers (rate-limit, cache).
3. Split the work: every teammate owns a clear part; list who built what in the submission form.
4. Code freeze is final: submit GitHub repo, app link and each teammate's role via the form.
   They demo from that link.

## Checklist for agents working on the build
- [ ] Every screen in the demo shows real data from at least one European public source.
- [ ] Both a buyer view and a supplier view exist.
- [ ] One deal goes end to end with no human click between "demand posted" and "deal closed".
- [ ] Nothing classified, export-controlled or customer-owned is fetched, stored or shown.
- [ ] Scrapers are rate-limited and cache responses.
- [ ] The app runs from the submitted link, not only on a teammate's laptop.
