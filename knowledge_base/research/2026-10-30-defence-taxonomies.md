# Defence product taxonomies for CPV 3531–3534 (weapons and ammunition)

Research notes for the TED marketplace. Written 30 October 2026.

Scope: CPV 35310000 Miscellaneous weapons, 35320000 Firearms, 35330000 Ammunition, 35340000 Parts of firearms and ammunition. Supplies only. Public sources only.

## How to read this

- Every fact has a source ID in square brackets. The IDs link to URLs in section 1.
- **V** = VERIFIED. I read the fact in the cited source.
- **V2** = VERIFIED in a secondary source only (aggregator, encyclopedia, or a search-result snippet of a page I could not open). Treat with care.
- **I** = INFERENCE. My own reasoning from the verified rules. Not stated as such in a source.
- **U** = UNVERIFIED. I looked and could not confirm it. Do not use as a code.
- I did not invent codes. If a cell says U, leave the field empty in the product data.

## Key takeaways

1. The weapon split that matters for licensing is **12.7 mm**. ML1 covers automatic weapons and "other arms" of 12.7 mm or less. ML2 covers weapons above 12.7 mm. Smooth-bore weapons split at **20 mm**. Ammunition for ML1 and ML2 weapons goes to **ML3**. Hand grenades, bombs, rockets, mines and military pyrotechnics go to **ML4**. [CML26]
2. FSC (NATO Supply Classification) splits by calibre band, not by weapon type: guns 1005 (≤30 mm), 1010 (>30–<75), 1015 (75–125), 1020, 1025, 1030, 1035. Ammunition follows the same bands: 1305, 1310, 1315, 1320. [PSC25]
3. The Firearms Directive does **not** apply to purchases by armed forces, police or public authorities (Art. 2(2)). Most TED buyers are exactly those. So the Directive category is a product attribute, not a buyer check. [FD]
4. The new import/export Regulation (EU) 2025/41 replaces Reg. 258/2012. It applies from **12 February 2029**, with some articles applying from 11 February 2025. Its Annex I already maps every Directive category to CN codes. Reuse that table. [R2541]
5. Most small-arms cartridges ship as **UN 0012, 1.4S**. 1.4S is transport category 4. That means "unlimited" quantity under the ADR 1.1.3.6 partial exemption. [ADR25]
6. Public STANAG numbers are confirmed for 5.56×45 (4172), 7.62×51 (2310), 9×19 (4090), 12.7×99 (4383), 5.7×28 (4509), 4.6×30 (4820), 25×137 (4173), 27×145 (3820), 30×173 (4624). Indirect fire (155 mm, 105 mm, mortars) is covered by STANAG 4425 and AOP-29. I could not confirm a calibre STANAG for 40 mm grenades or for the 120 mm mortar.

## 3. NATO Supply Classification / Federal Supply Classification

### 3.1 Structure

- The NATO Stock Number (NSN) has 13 digits. The first 4 digits are the NATO Supply Classification code (NSC), "also known as Group and Class". [MORH] **V**
- The first 2 digits are the group. The last 2 are the class. [MORH] **V**
- NATO adopted the US supply classification as its standard through STANAG 3150. [WIKI-NCS] **V2**
- The NSC codes are the same 4-digit codes as the US Federal Supply Class (FSC). [WIKI-NSC] **V2**
- The authoritative list is the DLA H2 handbook. [DLA-H2] (not opened). I used the US GSA PSC Manual FY2025 instead. Its product codes are updated "based on DLA Federal ... Class (FSC) list changes". [PSC25] **V**

### 3.6 "NATO class of supply" (Class V) – a different thing

- NATO also uses classes of supply I–V. Class V is ammunition, explosives and chemical agents. [WIKI-CoS] **V2**
- I could not open a NATO primary source for this. **U** (primary).
- Recommendation: store the 4-digit NSC/FSC as "NATO supply class". Store Class V as a derived flag for all group 13 items. **I**

## 4. EU Common Military List (CML)

### 4.1 Version

- Latest list: "Common Military List of the European Union", approved by the Council on **23 February 2026**. It updates and replaces the list of 24 February 2025. [CML26] **V**
- OJ reference: **OJ C, C/2026/1640, 13.3.2026**, ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/1640/oj. [CML26-OJ] **V** (cited by the Commission; I could not open EUR-Lex itself).
- Previous list: C/2025/1499, 6.3.2025. [CML26] **V** (footnote 1)
- The CML is the list of equipment covered by Council Common Position 2008/944/CFSP (exports of military technology and equipment). [CML26] **V**
- The Annex of Directive 2009/43/EC (intra-EU transfers of defence-related products) must "strictly correspond" to the CML. A delegated directive of 10 July 2026 aligns it with the 23 Feb 2026 CML. [CML26-OJ] **V**
- The CML is "a transposition of the Wassenaar Arrangement's Munitions List to EU law" and "is updated each time following updates to the Wassenaar Arrangement's Munitions List". [EEAS] **V**
- The ML1 and ML2 headings in the WA Munitions List of December 2025 read the same as in the CML 2026. [WA-ML], [CML26] **V** (my text comparison)

### 4.2 ML1 – small arms (≤ 12.7 mm) and smooth-bore < 20 mm

Heading: "Smooth-bore weapons with a calibre of less than 20 mm, other arms and automatic weapons with a calibre of 12.7 mm (calibre 0.50 inches) or less and accessories, as follows, and specially designed components therefor". [CML26] **V**

| Item | Text (short) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ML1.a | Rifles and combination guns, handguns, machine, sub-machine and volley guns | [CML26] V |
| ML1.b.1 | Smooth-bore weapons specially designed for military use | [CML26] V |
| ML1.b.2 | Other smooth-bore weapons: (a) fully automatic; (b) semi-automatic or pump-action | [CML26] V |
| ML1.c | Weapons using caseless ammunition | [CML26] V |
| ML1.d | Accessories for ML1.a–c: 1. detachable cartridge magazines; 2. sound suppressors or moderators; 3. 'gun-mountings' (fixture to mount a gun on a vehicle, aircraft, vessel or structure); 4. flash suppressors; 5. optical weapon-sights with electronic image processing; 6. optical weapon-sights specially designed for military use | [CML26] V |

Main notes and exclusions. [CML26] **V**
- ML1 does not apply to: (a) firearms for dummy ammunition that cannot fire a projectile; (b) tethered-projectile launchers with no HE charge or comms link, range ≤ 500 m; (c) **weapons using non-centre fire cased ammunition and which are not fully automatic** (i.e. non-automatic rimfire); (d) **'deactivated firearms'** (made irreversibly incapable of firing under national or WA-state processes).
- ML1.a does not apply to: rifles and combination guns made before 1938; reproductions of rifles whose originals predate 1890; handguns, volley guns and machine guns made before 1890 and their reproductions; compressed air / CO2 rifles or handguns; handguns for slaughtering or tranquilising animals.
- ML1.b does not apply to: smooth-bore weapons made before 1938; reproductions of pre-1890 originals; **smooth-bore weapons used for hunting or sporting purposes, if not specially designed for military use and not fully automatic**; smooth-bore weapons for slaughter, tranquilising, seismic testing, industrial projectiles, or IED disruption (N.B. disruptors: see ML4 and dual-use 1A006).
- ML1.b.2 does not apply to compressed air / CO2 smooth-bore weapons.

[CML26] **V**

### 4.4 ML3 – ammunition and fuze setting devices

Heading: "Ammunition and fuze setting devices, as follows, and specially designed components therefor". [CML26] **V**

- ML3.a: ammunition for weapons in ML1, ML2 or ML12. [CML26] **V**
- ML3.b: **fuze setting devices** specially designed for ML3.a ammunition. [CML26] **V**
- Note 1, components included: (a) metal or plastic fabrications such as primer anvils, bullet cups, **cartridge links**, rotating bands and munitions metal parts; (b) safing and arming devices, **fuzes**, sensors and initiation devices; (c) power supplies with high one-time output; (d) combustible cases for charges; (e) submunitions (bomblets, minelets, terminally guided projectiles). [CML26] **V**
- Note 2, ML3.a does not apply to: (a) ammunition crimped without a projectile (blank star); (b) dummy ammunition with a pierced powder chamber; (c) other blank and dummy ammunition without components designed for live ammunition; (d) components specially designed for such blank or dummy ammunition. [CML26] **V**
- Note 3, ML3.a does not apply to cartridges specially designed for (a) **signalling**, (b) bird scaring, (c) lighting gas flares at oil wells. [CML26] **V**

### 4.5 ML4 – bombs, grenades, rockets, mines, pyrotechnics, related equipment

Heading: "Bombs, torpedoes, rockets, missiles, other explosive devices and charges and related equipment and accessories, as follows, and specially designed components therefor". [CML26] **V**

- ML4.a: bombs, torpedoes, **grenades**, smoke canisters, rockets, mines, missiles, depth charges, demolition charges/devices/kits, **"pyrotechnic" devices**, cartridges, submunitions and **simulators**, **specially designed for military use**. [CML26] **V**
- ML4.a Note includes: (a) **smoke grenades**, fire bombs, incendiary bombs and explosive devices; (b) missile or rocket nozzles and re-entry vehicle nosetips. [CML26] **V**

- ML4.b: military equipment for handling, launching, laying, controlling, discharging, detonating, ... disposing of ML4.a items or IEDs. Note 2 excludes hand-held metal detectors that cannot tell mines from other metal. [CML26] **V**
- ML4.c: Aircraft Missile Protection Systems (with a civil-aircraft exclusion note). [CML26] **V**

## 5. Firearms Directive (EU) 2021/555 and the export/import regulations

### 5.1 Directive 2021/555 basics

- Title: Directive (EU) 2021/555 of 24 March 2021 on control of the acquisition and possession of weapons (codification). OJ L 115, 6.4.2021. It codifies Directive 91/477/EEC. [FD] **V**
- 'Firearm' = any **portable barrelled** weapon that expels, is designed to expel or may be converted to expel a shot, bullet or projectile by the action of a combustible propellant (Art. 1(1)(1)). [FD] **V**
- 'Essential component' = the barrel, the frame, the receiver (upper or lower), the slide, the cylinder, the bolt or the breech block. They take the category of the firearm they belong to (Art. 1(1)(2)). [FD] **V**
- 'Ammunition' = the complete round or its components (cartridge cases, primers, propellant powder, bullets or projectiles), **if those components are themselves subject to authorisation in the Member State** (Art. 1(1)(3)). [FD] **V**
- 'Alarm and signal weapons' = devices with a cartridge holder designed to fire only blanks, irritants, other active substances or pyrotechnic signalling rounds, and not convertible (Art. 1(1)(4)). [FD] **V**
- **Scope exclusion**: the Directive "shall not apply to the acquisition or possession of weapons and ammunition, in accordance with national law, by the armed forces, the police or the public authorities". Nor to transfers under Directive 2009/43/EC (Art. 2(2)). [FD] **V**

### 5.3 How the Directive treats ammunition and magazines

- Ammunition follows its firearm: "The arrangements for the acquisition and possession of ammunition shall be the same as those for the possession of the firearms for which the ammunition is intended" (Art. 13(1)). [FD] **V**
- Only A4 and A5 classify ammunition directly. [FD] **V**
- Magazines: "loading devices for centre-fire semi-automatic firearms which can hold more than 20 rounds or more than 10 rounds in the case of long firearms" may only be acquired by persons with an authorisation under Art. 9 or Art. 10(5) (Art. 13(1), second subparagraph). [FD] **V**
- Each elementary package of complete ammunition must be marked with manufacturer, batch/lot, calibre and type. [FD] **V**

## 7. ADR Class 1 (UN Model Regulations numbering)

ADR 2025 (ECE/TRANS/352) is the current edition. [ADR25] **V**

### 7.1 What the code means

- A classification code = division + compatibility group (2.2.1.1.4). [ADR25] **V**
- **Division 1.4**: "Substances and articles which present only a slight hazard of explosion in the event of ignition or initiation during carriage. The effects are largely confined to the package and no projection of fragments of appreciable size or range is to be expected." (2.2.1.1.5) [ADR25] **V**
- **Compatibility group S**: "Substance or article so packed or designed that any hazardous effects arising from accidental functioning are confined within the package unless the package has been degraded by fire, in which case all blast or projection effects are limited to the extent that they do not significantly hinder or prevent fire-fighting or other emergency response efforts in the immediate vicinity of the package." (2.2.1.1.6) [ADR25] **V**
- Other groups we meet: C = propellant; D = secondary detonating explosive, no initiator, no propelling charge; E = secondary explosive with propelling charge, no initiator; F = secondary explosive with its own initiator; G = pyrotechnic, illuminating, incendiary, tear or smoke; B = primary explosive article without two protective features. (2.2.1.1.6) [ADR25] **V**
- Classification is article-specific and based on tests. Assignment to n.o.s. entries is done by the competent authority of the country of origin. (2.2.1.1.3–2.2.1.1.4) [ADR25] **V**. So every UN number in the crosswalk is "typical", not guaranteed. **I**

### 7.2 What 1.4S means for carriers

- 1.4S goods are **transport category 4**. The 1.1.3.6.3 table gives "unlimited" as the maximum quantity for category 4. [ADR25] **V**
- Under 1.1.3.6.2, loads within that limit are exempt from, among others: Chapter 1.10 (security; except high-consequence Class 1), Chapter 5.3 (placarding and orange plates), 5.4.3 (instructions in writing), most of Chapter 7.2, CV1 of 7.5.11, most of Part 8, and Part 9 (vehicle construction). [ADR25] **V**
- Still required under that exemption: among others 8.1.2.1(a) (transport document on board), 8.1.4.2–8.1.4.5 (fire extinguishers), 8.2.3 (training), 8.3.3–8.3.5, Chapter 8.4, and S1(3), S1(6), S2(1), S4, S5, S14–S21, S24 of Chapter 8.5. [ADR25] **V**
- P130 is the packing instruction listed for UN 0012/0014. [ADR25] **V**. Package marking and labelling (Chapter 5.2) is not in the 1.1.3.6.2 exemption list, so it still applies. **I**
- UN 0012 and 0014 carry special provision 364 and a limited-quantity value of 5 kg. SP 364: the article "may only be carried under the provisions of Chapter 3.4 [limited quantities] if, as presented for carriage, the package is capable of passing the test in accordance with test series 6(d)". [ADR25] **V**
- Classes 1.4B to 1.4G are transport category 2 (333 kg/l threshold). [ADR25] **V**. So a 1.4C or 1.4G load gets much less relief than 1.4S. **I**

### 7.3 Glossary definitions that drive the family split (2.2.1.4)

- **CARTRIDGES, SMALL ARMS** (UN 0417, 0339, 0012): case with centre- or rim-fire primer, propelling charge and solid projectile, "designed to be fired in weapons of calibre not larger than **19.1 mm**. Shot-gun cartridges of any calibre are included." Some military small-arms cartridges are listed under CARTRIDGES FOR WEAPONS, INERT PROJECTILE instead. [ADR25] **V**
- **CARTRIDGES, SMALL ARMS, BLANK** (UN 0014, 0327, 0338): no projectile, calibre at most 19.1 mm. [ADR25] **V**
- **CARTRIDGES FOR WEAPONS, INERT PROJECTILE** (UN 0328, 0417, 0339, 0012): projectile without bursting charge but with propelling charge; may include a tracer. [ADR25] **V**
- **CARTRIDGES FOR WEAPONS with bursting charge**: UN 0006, 0321, 0412 = no means of initiation, or initiation with two or more protective features. UN 0005, 0007, 0348 = means of initiation **without** two protective features. Includes fixed, semi-fixed and separate-loading ammunition packed together. [ADR25] **V**
- **GRENADES, hand or rifle, with bursting charge**: "designed to be thrown by hand or to be projected by a rifle". UN 0284, 0285 = no initiator or two protective features; UN 0292, 0293 = initiator without two protective features. [ADR25] **V**
- **GRENADES, PRACTICE, hand or rifle** (UN 0110, 0372, 0318, 0452): no main bursting charge. [ADR25] **V**
- **PRIMERS, CAP TYPE** (UN 0044, 0377, 0378): "serve as igniting elements in small arms cartridges". [ADR25] **V**
- **PRIMERS, TUBULAR** (UN 0319, 0320, 0376): ignite the propelling charge in a cannon cartridge case. [ADR25] **V**

| EU CML / WA ML (weapons) | ML1 = automatic and "other arms" ≤ 12.7 mm, smooth-bore < 20 mm. ML2 = > 12.7 mm, smooth-bore ≥ 20 mm | V [CML26], [WA-ML] |
| EU CML (ammunition) | No cut. ML3.a covers ammo for ML1 and ML2 | V [CML26] |
| FSC | No cut at 12.7. 1005 and 1305 run "through 30mm" | V [PSC25] |
| ADR | "Small arms" = weapons of calibre ≤ 19.1 mm | V [ADR25] |
| NATO STANAG 4608 | Small-arms ammo safety: "below 12.7 mm"; Czech text says up to and including 12.7 mm | V [COS-130505] |
| Firearms Directive | No calibre cut. Uses portability and action type | V [FD] |

Recommendation: use **≤ 12.7 mm** for "small calibre". Put 14.5 mm in its own sub-family (AM-HMG / WP-HMG). The weapon is ML2, the ammo is ML3, FSC stays 1005/1305, and ADR may still treat the cartridge as "small arms". **I**

### 11.2 40 mm grenades: ammunition vs launchers

- Launcher: FSC 1010 names "Grenade Launchers for Integral-Cartridge Grenades". [PSC25] **V**. CN 9301 20 00 names "grenade launchers". [CN26] **V**. CML: ML2.a "projectile launchers" (calibre > 12.7 mm). [CML26] **V**
- Cartridge: FSC 1330 is for "Hand and Rifle Grenades" and excludes launchers. [PSC25] **V**. A 40 mm cartridge sits in 1310. **V2** (M433 NSN). CML sends grenade ammunition for ML1/ML2 weapons to ML3, not ML4. [CML26] **V**
- ADR: "GRENADES, hand or rifle" only covers grenades thrown by hand or projected by a rifle. [ADR25] **V**. So a 40 mm round is a "CARTRIDGE FOR WEAPONS with bursting charge" or "AMMUNITION, PRACTICE". **I**
- CN: 9306 30 30 vs 9306 90 10 is not settled by the text. **I**. Use a binding tariff information or the supplier's declared code.

### 11.3 Stun, smoke and irritant grenades

- FSC: all in 1330 (smoke grenades named; stun grenade by NSN example). [PSC25] **V**, M84 **V2**
- CML: smoke grenades are in ML4.a. Other grenades only if "specially designed for military use". RCA content falls under ML7.d unless packed for personal self-defence. [CML26] **V**. A police-only stun grenade may fall outside the CML and into national lists. **I**
- ADR: depends on the article. The M84 stun grenade is listed as UN 0297 1.4G, not as a grenade with bursting charge. **V2**
- CN: 9306 90 vs 3604 90 00 is unclear. **I**

### 11.4 Signals and pyrotechnics vs ammunition

- ML3 Note 3 excludes signalling cartridges. ML2.b Note excludes signal pistols. [CML26] **V**
- ML4.a includes military "pyrotechnic" devices and simulators. [CML26] **V**
- FSC: signal and flare guns in 1095; flares and signals in 1370; pyrotechnic cartridges and projectiles stay with ammunition (1370 excludes them; 1315 includes them). [PSC25] **V**
- CN: signalling flares go to chapter 36 (3604). Signal pistols stay in 9303. [CN26] **V**
- Firearms Directive: devices usable only for signalling are excluded from "firearm". [FD] **V**

### 11.5 Artillery and mortars inside CPV 3532

- CPV puts mortars, howitzers, AA, towed and SP artillery under 35322000 "Artillery", inside 35320000 "Firearms". [CPV] **V**
- They are ML2.a, CN 9301 10 00, FSC 1010–1035 by bore. [CML26], [CN26], [PSC25] **V**
- Under the Directive a "firearm" must be portable. [FD] **V**. Artillery is not portable. **I**. But Reg. 2025/41 still lists CN 9301 10 00 under Category A1. [R2541] **V**

### 11.6 Rockets: CPV 3533 vs FSC group 14

- CPV 35333200 Rockets is in scope; guided missiles are CPV 35600000. [CPV] **V**
- Unguided rockets map to FSC 1340, which excludes "rockets with built-in guiding devices". [PSC25] **V**. Do not use group 14 unless guided. **I**

### 11.7 Civilian vs military firearms

- Hunting and sporting smooth-bore guns are outside ML1.b and ML2.a, unless military-designed or fully automatic. [CML26] **V**
- Reg. 258/2012 does not apply to items "specially designed for military use" and to fully automatic firearms. [R258] **V**
- Reg. 2025/41 does not apply to Cat. A goods on the CML when exported, nor to goods for Member State armed forces, police or public authorities. [R2541] **V**
- Practical rule for TED: a public buyer means the Directive's acquisition rules do not bite on the buyer (Art. 2(2)). The **supplier** still needs a CML licence for intra-EU transfer (Directive 2009/43/EC) or export (CP 2008/944/CFSP) when the item is on the CML. [FD], [CML26-OJ], [CML26] **V** for each rule; the combined rule is **I**.

### 11.8 Deactivated, antique and replica items

- CML: deactivated firearms excluded (ML1 Note d). Pre-1938 rifles and pre-1890 handguns/MGs excluded. [CML26] **V**
- Directive: deactivated firearms are category C6. [FD] **V**
- Reg. 2025/41 lists deactivated firearms under ex 9304 00 00. [R2541] **V**
